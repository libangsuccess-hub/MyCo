# MYCO

"MyCo" is a student-focused learning and academic management platform designed to help students organize academic resources, manage their studies and engage in more personalized learning.

## The Problem

Students often have their lecture notes, textbooks, academic schedules, assessments and study tools scattered across different platforms.

MYCO aims to bring these academic activities into a more organized learning environment.

## Vision

To build an integrated learning platform that combines academic resource management, AI-assisted learning, academic tracking and study planning.

## Planned Features

- Academic resource organization.
- PDF and lecture-note management.
- AI-assisted learning.
- Learning assistance grounded in uploaded materials.
- Practice questions and mock examinations.
- CGPA calculation and academic tracking.
- Study planning and progress monitoring.
